# H.R. 4688

> To limit the use of nondisclosure agreements for employees of the White House or the Executive Office of the President, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Limitation on use of nondisclosure agreements for employees of the White House or the Executive Office of the President

- **(a)** *In general* An officer or employee of the White House or the Executive Office of the President may not be required to sign any nondisclosure agreement.
- **(b)** *Limitation on enforcement* No Federal funds may be used to enforce any nondisclosure agreement entered into by an officer or employee of the White House or the Executive Office of the President.
- **(c)** *Application* This Act shall not be construed to allow the disclosure of any information otherwise required by law, rule, or regulation to be kept secret in the interest of national security.
