# H.R. 7785

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend certain employment and reemployment rights to members of the National Guard who perform State active duty.

## § 1. Extension of certain employment and reemployment rights to members of the National Guard who perform State active duty

Section 4303 of title 38, United States Code, is amended in paragraph (13) by inserting “State active duty,” after “full-time National Guard duty,”.
