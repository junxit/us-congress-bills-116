# H.R. 1691

> To require the Secretary of Education to provide assistance to the immediate family of elementary or secondary school staff members killed in an act of violence while performing school duties.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Teacher Victims’ Family Assistance Act of 2019”.
