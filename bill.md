# H.R. 3445

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend the work geographic index floor under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Extension of the work geographic index floor under the Medicare program

Section 1848(e)(1)(E) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–4(e)(1)(E)) is amended by striking “2020” and inserting “2023”.
