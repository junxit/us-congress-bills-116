# H.R. 224

> To amend section 1908 of title 41, United States Code, to exempt certain contracts from the periodic inflation adjustments to an acquisition-related dollar threshold.

## § 1. Exemption of certain contracts from the periodic inflation adjustments to the acquisition-related dollar threshold

Subparagraph (B) of section 1908(b)(2) of title 41, United States Code, is amended by inserting “3131 to 3134,” after “sections”.
