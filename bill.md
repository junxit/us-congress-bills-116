# H.R. 3761

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to prohibit institutions of higher education from denying students access to transcripts because of loan default.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Access to Student Transcripts Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Prohibition on loss of access to transcripts for loan default

- **(a)** *In general* Section 487(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1094(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(30)**
  >   - **(A)** The institution will not prohibit a student from accessing the student’s transcripts, degree scrolls, or other certifications of coursework or educational attainments at the institution because the student is in default on a loan made, insured, or guaranteed under this title.
  >   - **(B)** For purposes of this paragraph, the term student includes former students.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect with respect to academic year 2019–2020 and each succeeding academic year.
