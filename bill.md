# S. RES. 707

> Recognizing September 1, 2020, as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”.

That the Senate—

- **(1)** recognizes September 1, 2020, as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”;
- **(2)** recognizes the need for, and appreciation of, the service of poll workers; and
- **(3)** encourages eligible people to help America vote in the 2020 election by serving as poll workers.
