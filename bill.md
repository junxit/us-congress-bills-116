# H.R. 3290

> To provide for mandamus actions under chapter 601 of title 49 of the United States Code.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “PHMSA Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Actions by private persons

Section 60121 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(e)** *Mandamus* A person may bring a civil action in an appropriate district court of the United States to compel the Secretary to perform a nondiscretionary duty under this chapter that the Secretary has failed to perform.
