# H.R. 6987

> To include payments for waste and recycling collection services as covered utility payments for purposes of calculating the eligible amount for forgiveness of indebtedness on a paycheck protection program loan.

## § 1. Inclusion of waste and recycling collection services

Section 1106(a)(5) of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act (Public Law 116–136) is amended by inserting “waste and recycling collection, or for” after “service for”.
