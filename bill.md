# H.J. RES. 90

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Community Reinvestment Act Regulations”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Community Reinvestment Act Regulations” (85 Fed. Reg. 34734; published June 5, 2020), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
