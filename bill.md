# H.R. 3764

> To amend title 9 of the United States Code to prohibit predispute arbitration agreements that force arbitration of disputes arising from private education loans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Justice for Student Borrowers Act”.

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to—

- **(1)** prohibit private educational lenders from using predispute arbitration agreements in their private education loans; and
- **(2)** to prohibit private educational lenders from using predispute joint-action waivers in their private education loans.
