# H.R. 5898

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to modify the exception from requirements for the operation of vehicles on certain highways in the State of Texas, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Agricultural Commerce Act”.

## § 2. Vehicle weight limitations-Interstate System

Section 127(n) of title 23, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “United States Route 83,” after “United States Route 281,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or Interstate Route 2” after “Interstate Route 69”.
