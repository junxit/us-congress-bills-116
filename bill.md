# S. 4105

> To impose a 1-year minimum prison sentence for the destruction of veterans' memorials.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Washington-Grant Historic Preservation Act”.

## § 2. Minimum sentence

Section 1369(a) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “shall be fined” and all that follows and inserting “shall be—

> - **(1)** fined under this title; and
> - **(2)** imprisoned for not less than 1 year and not more than 10 years.
