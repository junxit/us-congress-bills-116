> To provide for the conduct of certain water security measures in the State of New Mexico, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Rio Grande Compact* The term Rio Grande Compact means the compact approved by Congress under the Act of May 31, 1939 (53 Stat. 785, chapter 155).
- **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
- **(3)** *State* The term State means the State of New Mexico.
