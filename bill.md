# S. 234

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to require the disclosure of the annual percentage rates applicable to Federal student loans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transparency in Student Lending Act”.

## § 2. Required disclosures

Section 455(p) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087e(p)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “Each institution” and inserting the following:
  > - **(1)** *In general* Each institution

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** *Disclosure of APR prior to disbursement* In addition to the disclosures required under paragraph (1), each institution and contractor described in such paragraph shall disclose to a borrower before disbursement of a loan made under this part, in writing and in a form the borrower may keep, the annual percentage rate applicable to the loan, taking into account—
  >   - **(A)** the amount of the loan;
  >   - **(B)** the stated interest rate of the loan;
  >   - **(C)** the standard term for a loan of the same type;
  >   - **(D)** any fees or additional costs associated with the loan; and
  >   - **(E)** any capitalization of interest on the loan.
