# H.R. 3859

> To authorize the Attorney General to appoint 100 additional immigration judges, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Immigration judges

- **(a)** *In general* The Attorney General may appoint 100 additional immigration judges in addition to immigration judges currently serving as of the date of enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this section.
