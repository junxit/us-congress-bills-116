# H.R. 3560

> To provide assistance for the operation of the Lewis and Clark National Historic Trail Visitor Center in Nebraska City, Nebraska, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Lewis and Clark National Historic Trail operational assistance

Section 342(f) of Public Law 110–229 is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “, visitor center,” after “historic site”; and
- **(2)** by striking “per year for a period not to exceed 10 years” and inserting “for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2028”.
