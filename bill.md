# S. 2315

> To amend section 4712 of title 41, United States Code, to clarify the inclusion of subcontractors and subgrantees for whistleblower protection.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Whistleblower Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Protection against reprisal for Federal subgrantee employees

Section 4712 of title 41, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(2)(G), by striking “or grantee” and inserting “grantee, or subgrantee”;
- **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “contractor or grantee” and inserting “contractor, subcontractor, grantee, or subgrantee”;
- **(3)** in subsection (c)(1), by striking “contractor or grantee” each place it appears and inserting “contractor, subcontractor, grantee, or subgrantee”; and
- **(4)** in subsection (d), by striking “and grantees” and inserting “grantees, and subgrantees”.
