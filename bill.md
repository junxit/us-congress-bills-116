# H.R. 8200

> To improve the health of minority individuals during the COVID–19 pandemic, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ending Health Disparities During COVID–19 Act of 2020” or the “EHDC Act of 2020”.
