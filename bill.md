# H.R. 3870

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 511 West 165th Street in New York, New York, as the “Normandia Maldonado Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Normandia Maldonado Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 511 West 165th Street in New York, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Normandia Maldonado Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Normandia Maldonado Post Office Building”.
