# H.R. 3474

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to include truck combinations in the definition of automobile transporter for purposes of certain length limitations.

## § 1. Length limitations for automobile transporters

Section 31111(a)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by inserting “and truck tractors attached to trailers designed for the transportation of assembled highway vehicles” after “camper units”.
