# S. 725

> To change the address of the postal facility designated in honor of Captain Humayun Khan.

## § 1. Captain Humayun Khan Post Office

Section 1(a) of Public Law 115–347 (132 Stat. 5054) is amended by striking “180 McCormick Road” and inserting “2150 Wise Street”.
