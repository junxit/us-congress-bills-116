# S. 3050

> To amend the Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council Act of 1978 to provide designees of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs and the Administrator of the Rural Housing Service of the Department of Agriculture with positions on the Appraisal Subcommittee of the Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council.

## § 1. Appraisal Subcommittee

Section 1011 of the Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council Act of 1978 (12 U.S.C. 3310) is amended, in the first sentence, by inserting “the Department of Veterans Affairs, the Rural Housing Service of the Department of Agriculture,” after “Financial Protection,”.
