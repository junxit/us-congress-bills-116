# S. 3326

> To amend the Federal Credit Union Act to remove outdated responsibilities of boards of directors of Federal credit unions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Credit Union Modernization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Duties of boards of directors of Federal credit unions

The Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1751 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 111(b) (12 U.S.C. 1761(b)), in the second sentence, by striking “credit committee, and loan officers” and inserting “and credit committee”; and
- **(2)** in section 113 (12 U.S.C. 1761b)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “act upon applications for membership or”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (8), by striking “subchapter” and inserting “title”;
  - **(C)** by striking paragraph (10); and
  - **(D)** by striking paragraphs (15) and (16).
