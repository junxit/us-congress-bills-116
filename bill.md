# H.R. 1612

> To ensure election security, enhance Americans’ access to the ballot box, reduce the influence of big money in politics through transparency, establish accountability and integrity measures for Congress, and strengthen ethics rules for public servants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nonpartisan Bill For the People Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Organization of Act into divisions; table of contents

- **(a)** *Divisions* This Act is organized into 3 divisions as follows:
  - **(1)** Division A—Voting and Elections.
  - **(2)** Division B—Campaign Finance.
  - **(3)** Division C—Ethics.
