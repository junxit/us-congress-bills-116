# H.R. 3758

> To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to apportionments to small transit intensive cities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Community Transit Improvement Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Apportionments

Section 5336(h)(3) of title 49, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:
