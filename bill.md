# H.J. RES. 44

> Disapproving the final rule of the Occupational Safety and Health Administration titled “Tracking of Workplace Injuries and Illnesses”.

That Congress disapproves the final rule issued by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration titled “Tracking of Workplace Injuries and Illnesses” (84 Fed. Reg. 380) published on January 25, 2019, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
