# S. 85

> To reauthorize the Family Violence Prevention and Services Act.

## § 1. Family violence prevention and services

Section 303 of the Family Violence Prevention and Services Act (42 U.S.C. 10403) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(1), by striking “2011 through 2015” and inserting “2020 through 2024”;
- **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “2011 through 2015” and inserting “2020 through 2024”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (c), by striking “2011 through 2015” and inserting “2020 through 2024”.
