# H.R. 1834

> To direct the Secretary of Commerce to issue regulations prohibiting the use of sunscreen containing oxybenzone or octinoxate in a National Marine Sanctuary in which coral is present, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Defending Our National Marine Sanctuaries from Damaging Chemicals Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Prohibition on the use of oxybenzone and octinoxate

The Secretary of Commerce shall issue regulations prohibiting use, including application to another individual, of sunscreen containing oxybenzone or octinoxate in a National Marine Sanctuary in which coral is present.
