# H.R. 5441

> To repeal the funding authorization sunset and the total funding cap for the Essex National Heritage Area.

## § 1. Essex National Heritage Area

- **(a)** *Repeal of funding authorization sunset* Section 507 of division II of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Repeal of total funding cap* Section 508(a) of division II of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333) is amended by striking the last sentence.
