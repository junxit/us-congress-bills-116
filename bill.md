# H.R. 5504

> To amend the National Dam Safety Program Act with respect to the definition of eligible high hazard potential dam, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Eligible high hazard potential dam

Section 2(4)(B)(i) of the National Dam Safety Program Act (33 U.S.C. 467(4)(B)(i)) is amended by inserting “with an authorized installed capacity of greater than 1500 kilowatts” after “hydroelectric dam”.
