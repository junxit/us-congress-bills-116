# S. 1779

> To repeal the Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Equal Access to Justice for Victims of Gun Violence Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of the Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act

Sections 2, 3, and 4 of the Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act (15 U.S.C. 7901, 7902, and 7903) are repealed.
