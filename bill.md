# H.R. 5358

> To amend title 28, United States Code, to expand the definition of “other institutions” for purposes of acquisition, preservation, and exchange of identification records and information, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Definition of “other institutions” expanded

Section 534(e) of title 28, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** police departments serving census-designated private resorts which perform the administration of criminal justice and have arrest powers pursuant to a State statute, which allocate a substantial part of their annual budget to the administration of criminal justice, and which meet training requirements established by law or ordinance for law enforcement officers.
