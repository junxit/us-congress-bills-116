# H.R. 1923

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 1923) entitled “An Act to amend title 31, United States Code, to require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint and issue certain circulating collectible coins, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*At the end, add the following:*

## § 8. Cost

No coin or medal minted and issued under this Act, or an amendment made by this Act, may be sold at a price such that would result in a net cost to the Federal Government.
