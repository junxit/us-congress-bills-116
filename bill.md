# H.R. 2736

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to clarify that the estate of a deceased veteran may receive certain accrued benefits upon the death of the veteran, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans Estate Transfer to Survivors Act”.

## § 2. Clarification of eligible recipients of certain accrued benefits upon death of beneficiary

- **(a)** *Eligibility of estate* Section 5121(a)(2) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “, or estate,” after “person”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
    > - **(D)** The estate of the veteran (unless the estate will escheat).

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to the death of an individual on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
