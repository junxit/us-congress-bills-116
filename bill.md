# H. CON. RES. 75

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 3055.

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 3055, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** Amend the long title so as to read: “Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2020, and for other purposes.”.
- **(2)** Redesignate the second subsection (a) of section 1703 as subsection (b).
