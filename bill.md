# H.R. 1447

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to equalize the Federal medical assistance percentage applicable to certain individuals in the expansion population of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicaid Equality Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Equalizing the Federal medical assistance percentage for certain individuals in the expansion population of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act

Section 1905 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396d) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (y)(1)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (C), by adding “and” at the end;
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (D), by striking “; and” and inserting a period; and
  - **(C)** by striking subparagraph (E); and
- **(2)** in subsection (z)(2)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting “(through 2019)” after “thereafter”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B)(ii)(VI), by striking “and each subsequent year”.
