# H.R. 4496

> To extend indemnity for wildfires and hurricanes, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Wildfires and Hurricanes Indemnity Program Extension Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extension

Title I of subdivision 1 of division B of the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–123) is amended under the heading “Department of Agriculture—Agricultural Programs—Processing, Research and Marketing—Office of the Secretary”—

- **(1)** by striking “December 31, 2019” and inserting “December 31, 2021”;
- **(2)** by inserting “Florence, Michael,” after “Maria,”;
- **(3)** by inserting “, 2018, or 2019” after “occurring in calendar year 2017”; and
- **(4)** by inserting “, including disasters occurring in calendar year 2017, 2018, or 2019,” after “such losses”.
