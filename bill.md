# H.R. 4689

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to require that States give preference in allocating low-income housing credit dollar amounts to projects which are committed to providing non-smoking buildings.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Smoke Free Affordable Housing Act”.

## § 2. Low-income housing credit allocation preference for non-smoking buildings

- **(a)** *In general* Section 42(m)(1)(B)(ii) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new subclause:
  > - **(IV)** projects consisting only of buildings in which smoking is not permitted, and

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to allocations made after the date of the enactment of this Act.
