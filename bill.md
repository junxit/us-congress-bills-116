# H.R. 4589

> To make permanent the authority of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to make grants for the transportation of highly rural veterans to medical care.

## § 1. Permanent authority to make grants for transportation of highly rural veterans to medical care

Section 307(d) of the Caregivers and Veterans Omnibus Health Services Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–163; 124 Stat. 1154; 38 U.S.C. 1710 note) is amended by striking “of fiscal years 2010 through 2020” and inserting “fiscal year beginning with fiscal year 2010”.
