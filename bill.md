# S. 3753

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to ensure that certain medical facilities of the Department of Veterans Affairs have physical locations for the disposal of controlled substances medications.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “VA Directly Returning Opioid Prescriptions Act”.

## § 2. Requirement for certain Department of Veterans Affairs medical facilities to have physical location for the disposal of controlled substances medications

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall ensure that each covered Department medical facility has a physical location where patients may dispose of controlled substances medications.
- **(b)** *Covered Department medical facility* In this section, the term covered Department medical facility means a medical facility of the Department of Veterans Affairs with—
  - **(1)** an on-site pharmacy; or
  - **(2)** a law enforcement officer.
