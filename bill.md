# S. 4550

> To amend section 3142 of title 18, United States Code, to establish a rebuttable presumption that a person arrested for rioting or related offenses should be held pending trial.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Catch-and-Release for Rioters Act”.

## § 2. Rebuttable presumption on holding persons arrested for rioting pending trial

Section 3142(e)(3) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (D), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (E), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(F)** an offense under paragraph (1) or (2) of section 231(a) or under section 2101.
