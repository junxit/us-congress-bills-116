# S. 1806

> To make the E-Verify program permanent, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanent E-Verify Act”.

## § 2. Permanent authorization of E-Verify

Section 401 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 (8 U.S.C. 1324a note) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending the section heading to read as follows: “E-Verify Program”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** in the subsection heading, by striking “; termination”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “Unless the Congress otherwise provides, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall terminate a pilot program on September 30, 2015.”.
