# H.R. 7118

> To amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to require States to conduct elections for Federal office held in 2020 solely through the use of mail-in absentee ballots, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Vote From Home America Act of 2020”.
