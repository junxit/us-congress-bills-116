# S. RES. 719

> Recognizing September 22, 2020, as “National Voter Registration Day”.

That the Senate—

- **(1)** recognizes September 22, 2020, as “National Voter Registration Day”; and
- **(2)** encourages each voting-eligible citizen of the United States—
  - **(A)** to register to vote;
  - **(B)** to verify with the appropriate State or local election official that the name, address, and other personal information on record is current; and
  - **(C)** to cast a ballot in the election if the voting-eligible citizen would like to do so.
