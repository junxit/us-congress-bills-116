# S. 4179

> To update the blood donation public awareness campaign of the Department of Health and Human Services to include public awareness on plasma donation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Plasma Donation Awareness Act”.

## § 2. Importance of the blood and plasma supply

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3226 of the CARES Act (Public Law 116–136) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the section heading, by inserting “and plasma” after “blood”; and
  - **(2)** by inserting “and plasma” after “blood” each time such term appears.
