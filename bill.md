# H.R. 7833

> To authorize the Corps of Engineers to conduct a feasibility study for the project for comprehensive watershed study, Tuscarawas River Basin, Ohio.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tuscarawas River Flooding Study Act”.

## § 2. Tuscarawas River Basin, Ohio

The Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, is authorized to conduct a feasibility study for the project for comprehensive watershed study, Tuscarawas River Basin, Ohio.
