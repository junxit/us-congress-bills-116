# H.R. 8402

> To direct the Joint Committee on the Library to obtain a statue of Ruth Bader Ginsburg and to place the statue in the United States Capitol in National Statuary Hall.

## § 1. Placement of statue of Ruth Bader Ginsburg in National Statuary Hall

- **(a)** *Obtaining statue* Not later than 2 years after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Joint Committee on the Library shall enter into an agreement to obtain a statue or bust of Ruth Bader Ginsburg, under such terms and conditions as the Joint Committee considers appropriate consistent with applicable law.
- **(b)** *Placement* The Joint Committee shall place the statue or bust obtained under subsection (a) in the United States Capitol in a suitable permanent location in National Statuary Hall.
- **(c)** *Waiver of waiting period* Section 2(a) of Public Law 109–116 (2 U.S.C. 2131a(a)) does not apply with respect to the statue or bust obtained and placed under subsection (a).

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this Act, and any amounts so appropriated shall remain available until expended.
