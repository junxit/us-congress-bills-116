# S. 2480

> To amend title 31, United States Code, to reauthorize the payment in lieu of taxes program through fiscal year 2029.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “PILT Reauthorization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of payment in lieu of taxes program

Section 6906 of title 31, United States Code, is amended in the matter preceding paragraph (1) by striking “fiscal year 2019” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2019 through 2029”.
