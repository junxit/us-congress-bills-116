# H.R. 6372

> To extend the deadline for States to meet the requirements of the REAL ID Act of 2005.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “REAL ID Extension Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Extension of compliance deadline

The Secretary of Homeland Security shall grant to States an extension until September 30, 2021, to meet the requirements of section 202 of the REAL ID Act of 2005 (49 U.S.C. 30301 note; Public Law 109–13).
