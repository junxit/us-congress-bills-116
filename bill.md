# S. 3267

> To provide adequate information about excessive Federal spending, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Making Americans Know about Excessive spending through Commonsense Efforts to Notice and Target Shenanigans Act” or the “MAKE CENTS Act”.
