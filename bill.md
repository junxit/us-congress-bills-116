# H.R. 2242

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to include certain services in the definition of critical services, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Repair, restoration, and replacement of damaged facilities

Section 20601 of the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–123) is amended by inserting “, and including, for purposes of this section, solid waste management, stormwater management, public housing, transportation infrastructure, and medical care,” before “for the duration”.
