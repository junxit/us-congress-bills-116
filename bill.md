# H.R. 79

> To amend title 54, United States Code, to prohibit the extension or establishment of national monuments in Arizona except by express authorization of Congress, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Limitation on extension or establishment of national monuments in Arizona

Subsection (d) of section 320301 of title 54, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “or Arizona” after “Wyoming”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or Arizona” after “Wyoming”.
