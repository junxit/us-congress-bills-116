# H.R. 6981

> To extend the covered period for loan forgiveness and the rehiring period under the CARES Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

-  This Act may be cited as the “PPP Deadline Extension Adjustment for Loans Act” or the “PPP DEAL Act”.

## § 2. Extension of loan forgiveness period and rehiring period under CARES Act

Section 1106 of the CARES Act (Public Law 116–136) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(3), by striking “8-week” and inserting “16-week”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (d)(5)(B), by striking “June 30, 2020” each place that term appears and inserting “the date that is 16 weeks after the date of the origination of the covered loan”.
