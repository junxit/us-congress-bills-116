# H.R. 5916

> To require providers of health care products and services to publicly disclose point-of-sale cash prices.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transparent Health Care Pricing Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Transparency in all health care pricing

Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, all providers to the public of health care products and services shall publicly disclose on the internet and in print point-of-sale cash prices of such products and services.
