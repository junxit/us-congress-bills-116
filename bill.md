# S. 1516

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to strengthen the future workforce and reduce the cost of postsecondary education by reducing rates of postsecondary remediation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Readiness in Education to Prevent Additional Remediation and Expense Act ”.

## § 2. Reducing rates of postsecondary remediation
