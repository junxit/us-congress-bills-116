# S.J. RES. 30

> Providing for congressional disapproval of the proposed foreign military sale to the United Arab Emirates of certain defense articles and services.

That the following proposed foreign military sale to the United Arab Emirates is prohibited:

- **(1)** The sale of the following defense articles, including defense services and technical data, described in Transmittal No. 19–18, submitted to Congress pursuant to section 36(b) of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2776(b)) and published in the Congressional Record on June 4, 2019: The proposed sale of a blanket-order United States Marine Corps training, training support, and other training related services in support of the United Arab Emirates Presidential Guard Command.
