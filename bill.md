# H. RES. 1139

> Honoring the 50th Anniversary of the National Park Service Volunteers-In-Parks program that was established on July 29, 1970.

That the House of Representatives honors the 50th Anniversary of the National Park Service Volunteers-In-Parks program that was established on July 29, 1970.
