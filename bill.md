> To promote conservation, improve public land management, and provide for sensible development in Pershing County, Nevada, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *County* The term County means Pershing County, Nevada.
- **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
- **(3)** *State* The term State means the State of Nevada.
- **(4)** *Wilderness area* The term wilderness area means a wilderness area designated by section 301(a).
