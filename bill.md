# H.R. 7889

> To apply the Truth in Lending Act to small business financing, to regulate brokers and require the licensing of brokers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Lending Disclosure and Broker Regulation Act of 2020”.
