# S. 3318

> To promote transparency in health care pricing.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transparent Health Care Pricing Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Transparency in All Health Care Pricing

- **(a)** *In general* All providers to the public of health care items, products, services, or procedures shall disclose to the public, on a continuous basis, the cash prices for such items, products, services, or procedures in accordance with this section.
- **(b)** *Manner of disclosure* The disclosure required under subsection (a) shall—
  - **(1)** be made in an open and conspicuous manner; and
  - **(2)** be made available at the point of purchase, in print, and on the internet.
