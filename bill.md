# S. 4967

> To amend the CARES Act to extend the excise tax holiday period for aviation taxes.

## § 1. Extension of aviation excise tax holiday

Section 4007(c) of division A of the CARES Act is amended by striking “January 1, 2021” and inserting “January 1, 2022”.
