# H.R. 7970

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 183 San Geronimo Valley Drive in Woodacre, California, as the “Marylu Giddings Post Office”.

## § 1. Marylu Giddings Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 183 San Geronimo Valley Drive in Woodacre, California, shall be known and designated as the “Marylu Giddings Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Marylu Giddings Post Office”.
