# H.R. 6831

> To amend the CARES Act to authorize the Congressional Oversight Commission to submit discretionary reports to Congress, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Discretionary reports to Congress

Section 4020(b) of the CARES Act (Public Law 116–136) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(3)** *Discretionary reports to Congress* In addition to the reports required under paragraph (2), the Oversight Commission may submit other reports to Congress at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Oversight Commission determines appropriate.
