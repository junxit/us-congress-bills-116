# H.R. 8459

> To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to require organizations that spend more than $50,000 on Federal judicial nomination communications to file statements disclosing donors and certain other information with the Federal Election Commission.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Judicial Ads Act”.
