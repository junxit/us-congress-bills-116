# S. 1128

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gas emission fees, provide tax credits to workers, deliver additional benefits to retired and disabled Americans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Opportunity Carbon Fee Act of 2019”.
