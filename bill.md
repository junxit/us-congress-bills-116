# H.R. 6046

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Castle Pines, Colorado, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Castle Pines Community Act”.

## § 2. Single, unique ZIP Code for Castle Pines, Colorado

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code, to be numbered 80188, applicable to the area encompassing only Castle Pines, Colorado.
