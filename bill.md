# H.R. 4218

> To provide that Poland may be designated as a program country under the Visa Waiver Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Poland Visa Waiver Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Poland made eligible for the Visa Waiver Program

Notwithstanding any provision of section 217 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1187), the Secretary of Homeland Security may designate Poland as a program country under the visa waiver program established by that section.
