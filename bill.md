# H.R. 4778

> To amend the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008, to extend the authority to collect Shasta-Trinity Marina fees through fiscal year 2027.

## § 1. Shasta-Trinity Marina fees

Section 422 of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110–161; 121 Stat 2149) is amended by striking “2019'” and inserting “2027”.
