# H.R. 3173

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to require that institutions hosting a unit of the Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps provide instruction in STEM fields, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting STEM in JROTC Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2031(b)(3) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by inserting “and which includes instruction or activities in the fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics” after “duration”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
