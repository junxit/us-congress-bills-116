# H.R. 7790

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal chapter 35 (relating to taxes on wagering).

## § 1. Repeal

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 35 of subtitle D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2019.
