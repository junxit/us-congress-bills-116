# H.R. 677

> To amend gendered terms in Federal law relating to the President and the President’s spouse.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “21st Century President Act”.

## § 2. Modernization of terms relating to the President and the spouse of a President

Section 879(b)(1)(A) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “the wife of a former President during his lifetime, the widow of a former President until her death or remarriage” and inserting “the spouse of a former President during a former President’s lifetime, the surviving spouse of a former President until the surviving spouse’s death or remarriage”.
