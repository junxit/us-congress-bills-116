# S. 3273

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to establish a community college and career training grant program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Assisting Community Colleges in Educating Skilled Students to Careers Act of 2020” or the “ACCESS to Careers Act of 2020”.
