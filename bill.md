# H.R. 2363

> To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to employee protective arrangements, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Transit Modernization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Employee protective arrangements

- **(a)** *In general* Section 5333 of title 49, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “(a) Prevailing Wages Requirement.—”; and
  - **(2)** by striking subsection (b).
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments*
  - **(1)** *Formula grants for rural areas* Section 5311(i) of title 49, United States Code, is repealed.
  - **(2)** *Administrative provisions* Section 5334(a)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking “(except terms the Secretary of Labor prescribes under section 5333(b) of this title)”.
