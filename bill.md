# H.R. 2296

> To require reporting regarding certain drug price increases, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Accountability and Innovative Research Drug Pricing Act of 2019” or the “FAIR Drug Pricing Act of 2019”.
