# S.J. RES. 64

> Relating to the use of military force against the Islamic Republic of Iran.

## § 1. Use of military force against the Islamic Republic of Iran

Neither the Authorization for Use of Military Force (Public Law 107–40; 50 U.S.C. 1541 note) nor the Authorization for Use of Military Force Against Iraq Resolution of 2002 (Public Law 107–243; 50 U.S.C. 1541 note) may be interpreted as a statutory authorization for the use of military force against the Islamic Republic of Iran.
