# S. 4112

> To support education and child care during the COVID–19 public health emergency, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Coronavirus Child Care and Education Relief Act”.

## § 3. Definition of qualifying emergency

In this Act, the term qualifying emergency has the meaning given the term in section 3502 of division A of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act (Public Law 116–136).
