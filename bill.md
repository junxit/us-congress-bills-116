# H.R. 7799

> To designate a portion of United States Route 80 in Alabama as the “John R. Lewis Voting Rights Highway”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Renaming portion of United States Route 80

- **(a)** *Designation* The portion of United States Route 80 from Selma, Alabama to Montgomery, Alabama shall be known as the “John R. Lewis Voting Rights Highway”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the portion of United States Route 80 from Selma, Alabama to Montgomery, Alabama is deemed to be a reference to the “John R. Lewis Voting Rights Highway”.
