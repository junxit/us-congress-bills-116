# H.R. 879

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to modify the percentages of funds to be allocated to certain urbanized areas under the surface transportation block grant program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Support Local Transportation Act”.

## § 2. Surface transportation block grant program

Section 133(d)(6) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by striking subparagraphs (D) and (E) and inserting the following:

> - **(D)** for fiscal year 2019, 62.5 percent; and
> - **(E)** for fiscal year 2020, 65 percent.
