# H.R. 2943

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to make all fact sheets of the Department of Veterans Affairs in English and Spanish.

## § 1. Fact sheets

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall make versions of all fact sheets of the Department of Veterans Affairs in English and Spanish.
