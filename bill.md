# H.R. 1768

> To reauthorize subtitle G of title VII of the Energy Policy Act of 2005, relating to diesel emissions reduction, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Diesel Emissions Reduction Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 797(a) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 16137(a)) is amended by striking “2016” and inserting “2024”.
