# S. 2134

> To extend the transfer of Electronic Travel Authorization System fees from the Travel Promotion Fund to the Corporation for Travel Promotion (Brand USA) through fiscal year 2027.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Brand USA Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of funding for Brand USA

Subsection (d) of the Travel Promotion Act of 2009 (22 U.S.C. 2131(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(B), by striking “2020” and inserting “2027”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (4)(B), by striking “2020” and inserting “2027”.
