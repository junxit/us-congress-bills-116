# H.R. 5972

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 500 Delaware Avenue, Suite 1, in Wilmington, Delaware, as the “Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office Dedication Act”.

## § 2. Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 500 Delaware Avenue, Suite 1, in Wilmington, Delaware, shall be known and designated as the “Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Mary Ann Shadd Cary Post Office”.
