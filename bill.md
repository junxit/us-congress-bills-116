# S. 4298

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to make grants for improvements to meat and poultry facilities to allow for interstate shipment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Requiring Assistance to Meat Processors for Upgrading Plants Act” or the “RAMP–UP Act”.
