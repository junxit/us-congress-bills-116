# S. 2848

> To prohibit the expenditure of Federal funds to live stream border wall construction activities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Wall-Cam Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition against border wall cameras

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be expended to install or operate a video recording device at a border wall construction site for the purpose of live streaming the construction activities through a webcam.
