# H.R. 8917

> To amend the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 to extend the authorization of the South Carolina National Heritage Corridor, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of authorization of South Carolina National Heritage Corridor

Division II of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 607, by striking “2019” and inserting “2031”; and
- **(2)** in section 608(a), by striking “$17,000,000” and inserting “$30,000,000”.
