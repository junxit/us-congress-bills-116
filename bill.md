# H.R. 7783

> To amend the CARES Act to expand a national awareness and outreach campaign to address the importance and safety of plasma donation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Plasma Donation Awareness Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Importance of the blood and plasma supply

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3226 of the CARES Act (Public Law 116–136) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the section heading after “blood” by inserting “and plasma”; and
  - **(2)** by inserting after “blood” each time it appears “and plasma”.
