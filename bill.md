# S. 1438

> To amend title 39, United States Code, to extend the authority of the United States Postal Service to issue a semipostal to raise funds for breast cancer research.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Breast Cancer Research Stamp Reauthorization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extension of postage stamp for breast cancer research

Section 414(h) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking “2019” and inserting “2027”.
