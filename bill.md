# S. 862

> To repeal the sunset for collateral requirements for Small Business Administration disaster loans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rebuilding Small Businesses After Disasters Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of sunset for collateral requirements for SBA disaster loans

Section 2102 of the RISE After Disaster Act of 2015 (Public Law 114–88; 129 Stat. 690) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “(a) In general.—”; and
- **(2)** by striking subsections (b) and (c).
