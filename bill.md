# S. 3976

> To amend the Water Infrastructure Improvements for the Nation Act to extend the authorization of appropriations for the Lead Exposure Registry, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Flint Registry Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations for Lead Exposure Registry

Section 2203(d) of the Water Infrastructure Improvements for the Nation Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–27(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) and (2) as subparagraphs (A) and (B), respectively, and indenting appropriately;
- **(2)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A) (as so redesignated), by striking “There” and inserting the following:
  > - **(1)** *Fiscal years 2017 through 2021* There

- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** *Fiscal years 2022 through 2031* There is authorized to be appropriated for the period of fiscal years 2022 through 2031 $50,000,000 to carry out subsection (b).
