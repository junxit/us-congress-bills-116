# H.R. 8547

> To make permanent the tax deduction for energy efficient commercial buildings.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Incentivize Energy-Saving Improvements Act”.

## § 2. Tax deduction for energy efficient commercial buildings made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Section 179D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subsection (h).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2020.
