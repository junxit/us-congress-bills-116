# S. 3186

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to modify the definition of Appalachian region.

## § 1. Definition of Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(J) of title 40, United States Code, is amended by striking “and Spartanburg” and inserting “Spartanburg, and Union”.
