# H.R. 5207

> To amend section 235 of the Immigration and Nationality Act to remove authorization to implement the Migrant Protection Protocols, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “End the Migrant Protection Protocols Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Authorization to implement the Migrant Protection Protocols removed

Section 235(b)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1225(b)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “subparagraphs (B) and (C)” and inserting “subparagraph (B)” ; and
- **(2)** by striking subparagraph (C).
