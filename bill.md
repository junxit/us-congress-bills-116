# S. RES. 446

> Authorizing the printing of tributes and other related materials in honor of the late Senator Janet Kay Hagan.

That there be printed as a Senate document a compilation of tributes and other related materials concerning the Honorable Janet Kay Hagan, late a Senator from the State of North Carolina.
