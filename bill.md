# H.R. 3851

> To extend funding for Brand USA through fiscal year 2027, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Travel Promotion, Enhancement, and Modernization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Travel promotion fund

Subsection (d) of the Travel Promotion Act of 2009 (22 U.S.C. 2131) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(B), by striking “2020” and inserting “2027”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (4)(B), by striking “2020” and inserting “2027”.

## § 3. Electronic system for travel authorization fee increase

Section 217(h)(3)(B)(i)(I) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(I)) is amended by striking “$10” and inserting “$17”.
