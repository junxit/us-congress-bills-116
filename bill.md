# S. 2277

> To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 to make innovative technology loan guarantees available for battery storage technologies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Battery Storage Innovation Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Loan guarantees for battery storage technologies

Section 1703(b) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 16513(b)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(11)** Battery storage technologies for residential, industrial, or transportation applications.
