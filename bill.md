# H.R. 7108

> To amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to allow for emergency use of certain land during a pandemic, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Emergency use of CRP land during pandemic

Section 1233(b)(1)(B)(i)(I) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3833(b)(1)(B)(i)(I)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding item (aa), by striking “or other emergency,” and inserting “or other emergency (including a pandemic),”; and
- **(2)** in item (cc), by inserting “or pandemic” after “natural disaster event”.
