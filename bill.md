# H.R. 3054

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to modify the reductions in Medicaid DSH allotments.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “DSH Cuts Delay Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Modification of reductions in Medicaid DSH allotments

Section 1923(f)(7)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396r–4(f)(7)(A)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (i), in the matter preceding subclause (I), by striking “2020” and inserting “2022”; and
- **(2)** in clause (ii), by striking “equal to” and all that follows through the period and inserting “equal to $8,000,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2022 through 2025.”.
