# H.R. 4253

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to allow a recipient of an institutional aid grant to use funds under such grant to establish, improve, or expand partnerships with child care providers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Empower Parents in College Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Institutional aid grant authorized activities

Section 311(c) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1057(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating paragraph (13) as paragraph (14); and
- **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (12) the following:
  > - **(13)** Establishing, improving, or expanding partnerships with child care providers.
