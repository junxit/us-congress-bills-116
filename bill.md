# H.R. 4196

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to include child development and early learning as community services under the Federal work-study program.

## § 1. Child development and early learning community services

Section 441(c)(1) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087–51(c)(1)) is amended by striking “literacy training” and inserting “child development and early learning (including Head Start programs and Early Head Start programs carried out under the Head Start Act (42 U.S.C. 9831 et seq.), literacy training”.
