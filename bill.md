# H.R. 5171

> To permanently authorize the National Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reauthorization of the National Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity Act”.

## § 2. National Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity

Section 114 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1011c) is amended by striking subsection (f).
