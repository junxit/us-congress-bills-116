# H.R. 8996

> To amend title 46, United States Code, to repeal provisions relating to transportation by water that are popularly known as the Jones Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Jones Act Repeal Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of provisions popularly known as the Jones Act

Title 46, United States Code, is amended by repealing the following provisions:

- **(1)** Chapter 551, and the item relating to that chapter in the analysis at the beginning of part D of subtitle V of that title.
- **(2)** Section 12102, and the item relating to that section in the analysis at the beginning of chapter 121 of that title.
