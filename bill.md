# H. CON. RES. 107

> Directing the Clerk of the House to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 4.

That in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 4, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: In the short title, strike “Voting Rights Advancement Act of 2019” and insert the following: “John R. Lewis Voting Rights Act of 2020”.
