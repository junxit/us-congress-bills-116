# S. 4763

> To require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to submit to Congress a report on the Veteran Engagement Through Electronic Resources and Notifications Study conducted by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veteran Engagement Through Electronic Resources and Notifications Study Act of 2020” .

## § 2. Report on Veteran Engagement Through Electronic Resources and Notifications Study

Not later than September 30, 2022, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall submit to Congress a report on the Veteran Engagement Through Electronic Resources and Notifications Study conducted by the Department of Veterans Affairs.
