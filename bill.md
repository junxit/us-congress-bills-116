## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Fund* The term “Fund” means the Bureau of Reclamation Infrastructure Fund established by section 102(a).
- **(2)** *Reclamation state* The term Reclamation State has the meaning given the term in section 4014 of the Water Infrastructure Improvements for the Nation Act (43 U.S.C. 390b note; Public Law 114–322).
- **(3)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
