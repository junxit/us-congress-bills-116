# S. 3079

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to provide family and medical leave to employees of the Federal Aviation Administration and the Transportation Security Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “FAA and TSA Family and Medical Leave Act”.

## § 2. Family and medical leave for FAA and TSA employees

Section 40122(g)(2) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (I)(iii), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (J), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(K)** subchapter V of chapter 63, relating to family and medical leave.
