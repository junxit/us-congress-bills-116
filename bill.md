# H.R. 6784

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to repeal the provision relating to private correspondence with foreign governments, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Time to Repeal an Archaic Policy Act of 2020” or the “TRAP Act of 2020” .

## § 2. Repeal of provision relating to private correspondence with foreign governments

- **(a)** *Repeal* Section 953 of title 18, United States Code, is repealed.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* The table of sections at the beginning of chapter 45 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking the item relating to section 953.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The repeal made by subsection (a) shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
