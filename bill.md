# H.R. 7802

> To require the Planned Parenthood Federation of America and any affiliates to return amounts received under the paycheck protection program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Return of paycheck protection program amounts

Not later than 30 days after the date of the enactment of this Act and upon request by the Secretary of the Treasury, the Planned Parenthood Federation of America (and any affiliates) shall return any amounts received from a covered loan made under section 7(a)(36) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)(36)).
