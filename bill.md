# H.R. 6407

> To rescind the appropriation made to the Kennedy Center in the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act.

## § 1. Rescinding appropriation for Kennedy Center in CARES Act

- **(a)** *In general* The Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act is amended by striking the item relating to the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts.
- **(b)** *Effective date* Subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in the enactment of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act.
