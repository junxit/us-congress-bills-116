# H.R. 2119

> To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 to reauthorize grants for improving the energy efficiency of public buildings, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Energy efficient public buildings

Section 125(c) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 15822(c)) is amended by striking “$30,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2006 through 2010” and inserting “$100,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2021 through 2025”.
