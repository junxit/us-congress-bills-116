# H.R. 2427

> To amend the Chesapeake Bay Initiative Act of 1998 to reauthorize the Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails Network.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails Network Reauthorization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails Network

Section 502(c) of the Chesapeake Bay Initiative Act of 1998 (54 U.S.C. 320101 note; Public Law 105–312) is amended by striking “2019” and inserting “2025”.
