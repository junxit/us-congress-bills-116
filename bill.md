# S.J. RES. 55

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Acting Secretary of Homeland Security relating to “Immigrant Investor Program Modernization”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Acting Director of Homeland Security relating to “Immigrant Investor Program Modernization” (84 Fed. Reg. 35750; July 24, 2019), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
