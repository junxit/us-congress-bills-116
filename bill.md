# S. 1367

> To designate Union Station in Washington, DC, as “Harry S. Truman Union Station”.

## § 1. Harry S. Truman Union Station

- **(a)** *Designation* Union Station, located at 40 Massachusetts Avenue, NE, Washington, DC, shall be known and designated as “Harry S. Truman Union Station”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Harry S. Truman Union Station”.
