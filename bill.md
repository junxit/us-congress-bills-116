# H.R. 152

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 810 South Pendleton Street in Easley, South Carolina, as the “Captain Kimberly Nicole Hampton Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Captain Kimberly Nicole Hampton Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 810 South Pendleton Street in Easley, South Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Captain Kimberly Nicole Hampton Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Captain Kimberly Nicole Hampton Post Office Building”.
