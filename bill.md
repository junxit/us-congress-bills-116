# S. 4726

> To amend the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 to require the Secretary of Transportation to develop and carry out a program to improve energy efficiency in transportation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Better Utilizing Innovative Low-emission Development Strategies Act” or the “BUILDS Act”.
