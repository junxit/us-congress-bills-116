# S. 2176

> To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for temporary protected status.

## § 1. Designation of Haiti for temporary protected status

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall designate Haiti for temporary protected status for a period of 18 months beginning on July 22, 2019.
