# S. 4960

> To extend of period for adjustment of status for certain Liberian nationals.

## § 1. Extension of period for adjustment of status for certain Liberian nationals

Section 7611(b)(1)(A) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 (Public Law 116–92; 133 Stat. 2309; 8 U.S.C. 1255 note) is amended by striking “1 year” and inserting “June 30, 2021”.
