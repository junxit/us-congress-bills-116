# H.R. 1624

> To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to prohibit contributions and donations by foreign nationals in connection with State or local ballot initiatives or referenda.

## § 1. Prohibition on contributions and donations by foreign nationals in connection with ballot initiatives and referenda

- **(a)** *In general* Section 319(a)(1)(A) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (52 U.S.C. 30121(a)(1)(A)) is amended by striking “election;” and inserting the following: “election, including a State or local ballot initiative or referendum;”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this Act shall apply with respect to elections held in 2020 or any succeeding year.
