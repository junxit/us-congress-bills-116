# H.R. 3582

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand the scope of the Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Expansion of scope of Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans

Section 544(d) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (4), by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon;
- **(2)** in paragraph (5), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(4)** lesbian, gay, bisexual, or transgender.
