# H.R. 7122

> To require the Secretary of the Treasury to provide estimates of the use of taxpayer funds by the United States Government, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Taxpayer Receipt Act”.

## § 2. Use of taxpayer funds

The Secretary of the Treasury, or the Secretary’s delegate, shall provide, to each individual filing a Federal income tax return for a calendar year, a one-page estimate of how the taxpayer’s money was spent by the Government during the immediately preceding calendar year.
