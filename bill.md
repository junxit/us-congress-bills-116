# H.R. 1146

> To amend Public Law 115–97 (commonly known as the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act) to repeal the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge oil and gas program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Arctic Cultural and Coastal Plain Protection Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of Arctic National Wildlife Refuge oil and gas program

Section 20001 of Public Law 115–97 (16 U.S.C. 3143 note) is hereby repealed.
