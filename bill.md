# H.R. 72

> To provide that the final rule of the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection titled “Home Mortgage Disclosure (Regulation C)” shall have no force or effect.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Let Lenders Lend Act”.

## § 2. Home mortgage disclosure rule

The final rule issued by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection titled “Home Mortgage Disclosure (Regulation C)” (October 28, 2015; 80 Fed. Reg. 66128) shall have no force or effect.
