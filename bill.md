# H.R. 7861

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the employer credit for paid family and medical leave.

## § 1. Employer credit for paid family and medical leave made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Section 45S of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subsection (i).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to wages paid in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2020.
