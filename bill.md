# H.R. 552

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Eastvale, California.

## § 1. Single, unique zip code for Eastvale, California

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this section, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code applicable to the area encompassing only Eastvale, California.
