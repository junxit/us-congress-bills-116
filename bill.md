# H.R. 4261

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds for payments in support of campaigns for election for the offices of Senator or Representative in Congress.

## § 1. Prohibiting use of Federal funds for payments in support of congressional campaigns

No Federal funds may be used to make any payment in support of a campaign for election for the office of Senator or Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress.
