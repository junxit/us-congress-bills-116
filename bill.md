# H.R. 1638

> To amend title 32, United States Code, to authorize the transfer of equipment and facilities from the Federal Government or a State, Tribal, or local government to the National Guard for purposes of the National Guard Youth Challenge Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Building Success for Youth ChalleNGe Act”.

## § 2. National Guard Youth Challenge Program: equipment and facilities

Section 509(h) of title 32, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating paragraph (2) as paragraph (4); and
- **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (1) the following new paragraphs:
  > - **(2)** Equipment and facilities of the United States may be transferred to the National Guard for purposes of carrying out the Program.
  > - **(3)** Equipment and facilities of a State, Tribal, county, or local government entity may be transferred to the National Guard for purposes of carrying out the Program.
