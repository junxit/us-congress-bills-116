# S. 4599

> To withdraw certain Federal land in the Pecos Watershed area of the State of New Mexico from mineral entry, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Pecos Watershed Protection Act”.

## § 2. Withdrawal of Federal land in Pecos Watershed area, New Mexico

- **(a)** *Definition of Federal land* In this section, the term Federal land means the Federal land depicted as “Pecos Withdrawal” on the map entitled “Pecos Watershed, upstream of I–25 Mineral Withdrawal” and dated September 1, 2020.
- **(b)** *Withdrawal* Subject to valid rights in existence on the date of enactment of this Act, the Federal land is withdrawn from all forms of—
  - **(1)** entry, appropriation, or disposal under the public land laws;
  - **(2)** location, entry, and patent under the mining laws; and
  - **(3)** disposition under all laws pertaining to mineral and geothermal leasing or mineral materials.
