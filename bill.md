# H. RES. 1055

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make corrections in the engrossment of H.R. 6395.

That in the engrossment of the bill H.R. 6395, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: In section 5001 (relating to Department of State authorities and activities), strike “Department of State Authorization Act of 2020” and insert “Eliot L. Engel Department of State Authorization Act of 2020”.
