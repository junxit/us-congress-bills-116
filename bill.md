# S. 3813

> To amend the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 to reauthorize the Ohio & Erie National Heritage Canalway, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Ohio & Erie National Heritage Canalway

Division II of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333; 110 Stat. 4274; 122 Stat. 824; 122 Stat. 826; 127 Stat. 3801; 127 Stat. 420; 128 Stat. 314; 133 Stat. 778) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 809, by striking “2021” and inserting “2036”; and
- **(2)** in section 810(a), by striking “$20,000,000” and inserting “$30,000,000”.
