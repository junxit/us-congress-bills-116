# H.R. 6654

> To amend title XI of the Social Security Act to ensure the extension of the telehealth waiver authority of the Secretary of Health and Human Services to physical therapists, occupational therapists, speech language-pathologists, and certain other providers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency COVID Telehealth Response Act”.
