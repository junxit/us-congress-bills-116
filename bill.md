# H.R. 5384

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 100 Crosby Street in Mansfield, Louisiana, as the “Dr. C.O. Simpkins, Sr., Post Office”.

## § 1. Dr. C.O. Simpkins, Sr., Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 100 Crosby Street in Mansfield, Louisiana, shall be known and designated as the “Dr. C.O. Simpkins, Sr., Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Dr. C.O. Simpkins, Sr., Post Office”.
