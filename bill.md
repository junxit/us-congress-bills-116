# H.R. 8080

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to encourage the development of certain public health data standards, authorize epidemiological surveillance grants, and authorize a data linkage demonstration project, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Health Standards To Advance Transparency, Integrity, Science, Technology Infrastructure, and Confidential Statistics Act of 2020” or the “Health STATISTICS Act of 2020”.
