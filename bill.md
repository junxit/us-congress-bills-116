# H.R. 8765

> To codify the Industry-Recognized Apprenticeship Programs of the Department of Labor.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Modernizing America’s Apprenticeship Act”.

## § 2. Industry-Recognized Apprenticeship Programs of the Department of Labor

The provisions of part 29 of title 29, Code of Federal Regulations, as amended by the final regulations entitled “Apprenticeship Programs, Labor Standards for Registration, Amendment of Regulations” published by the Department of Education in the Federal Register on March 11, 2020 (85 Fed. Reg. 14294 et seq.), and as in effect on the day before the date of enactment of this Act, shall have the force and effect of enacted law.
