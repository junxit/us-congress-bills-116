# H.R. 540

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 770 Ayrault Road in Fairport, New York, as the “Louise and Bob Slaughter Post Office”.

## § 1. Louise and Bob Slaughter Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 770 Ayrault Road in Fairport, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Louise and Bob Slaughter Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Louise and Bob Slaughter Post Office”.
