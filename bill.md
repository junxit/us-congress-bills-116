# H.R. 9067

> To establish an office within the Department of Commerce to coordinate all non-defense related deployment and activities related to blockchain technology within the Federal Government.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Blockchain Technology Coordination Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Establishment of national blockchain technology coordination office

There is established within the Department of Commerce a National Blockchain Technology Coordination Office (in this Act referred to as the “Office”) which shall be organized and led by a Director, who shall be appointed by the Secretary not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
