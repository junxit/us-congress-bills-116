# H.R. 5452

> To provide a duplication of benefits fix for Sandy CDBG–DR recipients, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sandy Duplication of Benefits Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Duplication of benefits

- **(a)** *Amendment* Section 1210(a)(3) of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–254; 132 Stat. 3186) is amended by striking “January 1, 2016” and inserting “October 1, 2012”.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in the enactment of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–254; 132 Stat. 3186).
