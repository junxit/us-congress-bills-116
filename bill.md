# H.R. 3612

> To repeal a section of the FAST Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Repeal

Section 1438 of the FAST Act (Public Law 114–94; 129 Stat. 1432), and the item relating to that section in section 1(b) of that Act, are repealed.
