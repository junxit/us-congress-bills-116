# S. 702

> To amend the Older Americans Act of 1965 to establish an initiative, carried out by the Assistant Secretary for Aging, to coordinate Federal efforts and programs for home modifications enabling older individuals and individuals with disabilities to live independently and safely in a home environment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Senior and Disability Home Modification Assistance Initiative Act”.
