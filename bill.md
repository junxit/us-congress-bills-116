# H.R. 4521

> To reform the Federal sugar program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Sugar Policy Act of 2019” .

## § 2. Sugar program

Paragraph (5) of section 156(a) of the Federal Agriculture Improvement and Reform Act of 1996 (7 U.S.C. 7272(a)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(5)** 18.75 cents per pound for raw cane sugar for each of the 2020 through 2024 crop years.
