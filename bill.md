# H.R. 2695

> To rename the Success Dam in Tulare County, California, as the Richard L. Schafer Dam.

## § 1. Renaming of dam

- **(a)** *Renaming* The Success Dam in Tulare County, California, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Richard L. Schafer Dam”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the dam referred to in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Richard L. Schafer Dam.
