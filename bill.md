# H.R. 8833

> To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to extend by one year certain port of entry donation authority, and for other purposes.

## § 1. One-year extension of certain port of entry donation authority

Subparagraph (A) of section 482(b)(4) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 301a(b)(4)) is amended by striking “4” and inserting “5”.
