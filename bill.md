# H. CON. RES. 128

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 1520.

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 1520 the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

Amend the title so as to read: “Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2021, and for other purposes.”.
