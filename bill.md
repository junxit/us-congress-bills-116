# S. 4086

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to revise the definition of Vietnam era for purposes of the laws administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Forgotten Vietnam Veterans Act of 2020”.

## § 2. Revision of definition of Vietnam era for purposes of the laws administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs

Section 101(29)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “February 28, 1961” and inserting “November 1, 1955”.
