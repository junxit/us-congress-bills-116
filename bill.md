# H.R. 8896

> To repeal section 230 of the Communications Act of 1934 (commonly referred to as the Communications Decency Act) to stop censorship, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Abandoning Online Censorship Act” or the “AOC Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of section 230 of the Communications Act of 1934 (commonly referred to as the Communications Decency Act)

- **(a)** *Section 230 of the Communications Act of 1934* Effective as of enactment of this language, section 230 of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 230(c)) is repealed.
