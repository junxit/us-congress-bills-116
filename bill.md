# S. RES. 3

> To elect Chuck Grassley, a Senator from the State of Iowa, to be President pro tempore of the Senate of the United States.

That Chuck Grassley, a Senator from the State of Iowa, be, and he is hereby, elected President of the Senate pro tempore.
