# H.R. 4562

> To amend the exemption of certain legal requirements applied to Amtrak in making improvements to exclude the construction of advertisements.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Allowing More Transparency in Reviewing Advertising Construction Act” or the “AMTRAK Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on exemption for Amtrak from certain legal requirements

Section 24902(j) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(k)** *Limitation on exemptions* Subsection (j) shall not apply to an improvement activity carried out for the purpose of construction or display of an advertisement.
