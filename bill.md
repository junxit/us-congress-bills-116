# H.R. 4414

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to lower the cost of college education by establishing pilot programs to expand student access to digital course materials.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Electronic Books Opening Opportunity for Knowledge Act of 2019” or the “E–BOOK Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to identify savings in the cost of higher education for undergraduate students by funding pilot programs to expand student access to digital academic course materials.
