# H.R. 2134

> To reauthorize the Helen Keller National Center for Youths and Adults Who Are Deaf-Blind.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Helen Keller National Center Reauthorization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Helen Keller National Center reauthorized

The first sentence of section 205(a) of the Helen Keller National Center Act (29 U.S.C. 1904(a)) is amended by striking “1999 through 2003” and inserting “2019 through 2023”.
