# S. 1110

> To provide for restoration, economic development, recreation, and conservation on Federal lands in Northern California, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Northwest California Wilderness, Recreation, and Working Forests Act”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means—
  - **(A)** with respect to land under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of Agriculture; and
  - **(B)** with respect to land under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of the Interior.
- **(2)** *State* The term State means the State of California.
