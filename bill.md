# H.R. 4308

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to establish an emergency grant aid program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cost Assistance Made Possible for Undergraduate Students Act” or the “CAMPUS Act”.
