# S. 1241

> To expand the private right of action under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act for calls in violation of the Do Not Call rules.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting American Consumers from Robocalls Act”.

## § 2. Private right of action for calls in violation of Do Not Call rules

Section 227(c)(5) of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 227(c)(5)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “more than one telephone call within any 12-month period by or on behalf of the same entity” and inserting “a telephone call by or on behalf of an entity”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “up to”.
