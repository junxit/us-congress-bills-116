# H.R. 3644

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend the Independence at Home medical practice demonstration program under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Independence at Home Demonstration Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extending the Independence at Home medical practice demonstration program under the Medicare program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1866E(e)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395cc–5(e)(1)) is amended by striking “7-year” and inserting “10-year”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in the enactment of Public Law 111–148.
