# H.R. 430

> To extend the program of block grants to States for temporary assistance for needy families and related programs through June 30, 2019.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “TANF Extension Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extension of the Temporary Assistance for Needy Families Program and related programs through June 30, 2019

Activities authorized by part A of title IV and section 1108(b) of the Social Security Act shall continue through June 30, 2019, in the manner authorized for fiscal year 2018, and out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, there are hereby appropriated such sums as may be necessary for such purpose.
