# H.R. 3053

> To amend title V of the Social Security Act to reauthorize the Personal Responsibility Education Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be referred to as the “PREP Reauthorization Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extension of Personal Responsibility Education Program (PREP)

Section 513 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 713) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraphs (1)(A) and (4)(A) of subsection (a), by striking “2019” and inserting “2024” each place it appears;
- **(2)** in subsection (a)(4)(B)(i), by striking “2019” and inserting “2024”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (f), by striking “2019” and inserting “2024”.
