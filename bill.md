# H.R. 1832

> To establish minimum standards relating to a factor for life insurance eligibility and rates, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Insurance Fairness for First Responders Act of 2019”.

## § 3. Timing of regulations

The Secretary of the Treasury shall issue the regulations required by section 555 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, as added by section 2 of this Act, not later than the expiration of the 6-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.
