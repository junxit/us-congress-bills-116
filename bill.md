# H.R. 1326

> To provide for the preservation of America’s outdoor heritage and enhance recreation opportunities on Federal land, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; Secretary defined; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Authorizing Critical Conservation and Enabling Sportsmen and Sportswomen Act” or the “ACCESS Act”.
- **(b)** *Secretary defined* For the purposes of this Act, the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Interior unless otherwise specifically indicated.
