# H.R. 1176

> To repeal the restriction on the use of funds by the Securities and Exchange Commission to ensure that shareholders of corporations have knowledge of corporation political activity.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transparency in Corporate Political Spending Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of restriction on use of funds by Securities and Exchange Commission to ensure shareholders of corporations have knowledge of corporation political activity

Notwithstanding section 101 of division C of Public Law 115–245, section 631 of Division E of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2018 shall have no force or effect during fiscal year 2019.
