# H.R. 6513

> To defer the year of inclusion for certain capital gains invested in a qualified opportunity fund.

## § 1. Deferral of gain invested in opportunity zone property extended to 2030

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1400Z–2(b)(1)(B) is amended by striking “December 31, 2026” and inserting “December 31, 2030”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of enactment of this section.
