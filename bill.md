# H.R. 3043

> To amend section 6906 of title 31, United States Code, to permanently authorize the Payment in Lieu of Taxes program.

## § 1. Payment in lieu of taxes

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Permanently Authorizing PILT Act”.
- **(b)** *Permanent authorization* Section 6906 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking “fiscal year 2019” and inserting “each fiscal year”.
