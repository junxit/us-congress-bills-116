# S. 4165

> To repeal section 692 of the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006.

## § 1. Repeal

Section 692 of the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006 (6 U.S.C. 792) is repealed.
