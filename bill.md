# S. 2489

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority for financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keeping Our Commitment to Ending Veteran Homelessness Act of 2019”.

## § 2. Extension of authority for financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing

Section 2044(e)(1)(G) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “fiscal year 2019” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2019 through 2021”.
