# H.R. 8493

> To amend the Passport Act of 1920 to exempt from the collection of certain passport fees an individual who was awarded the Purple Heart, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Passports for Purple Hearts Act”.

## § 2. Exemption from certain passport fees of an individual who was awarded the Purple Heart

Section 1 of the Act of June 1, 1920 (22 U.S.C. 214; commonly referred to as the “Passport Act of 1920”), is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), in the third sentence, by inserting “from an individual who was awarded the Purple Heart;” before “or from an individual or individuals abroad”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(c)** In this section, the term “passport” includes a passport card.
