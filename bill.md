# S. 1921

> To provide that primary care services provided by the National Health Service Corps may include palliative care services.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Provider Training in Palliative Care Act”.

## § 2. Palliative care

Section 331(a)(3)(D) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254d(a)) is amended by inserting “palliative care,” after “gynecology,”.
