# S. 1653

> To amend section 3063 of title 18, United States Code, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Powers of the Environmental Protection Agency

Section 3063 of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking paragraph (1); and
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraphs (2) and (3) as paragraphs (1) and (2), respectively; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(c)** The guidelines approved under subsection (b) shall require that a law enforcement officer described in subsection (a) provide notice to appropriate State and local law enforcement agencies before executing and serving any warrant.
