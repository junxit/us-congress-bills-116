# H.R. 6852

> To prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System from waiving provisions related to COVID–19 emergency relief and taxpayer protections related to such relief.

## § 1. Prohibition on waivers

Section 4003(c)(3)(A)(iii) of the CARES Act (15 U.S.C. 9042(c)(3)(A)(iii)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(iii)** *Prohibition on waivers* The Secretary and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System may not waive any requirement under this section or of a rule issued to carry out this section.
