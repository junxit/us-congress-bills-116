# H.R. 4171

> To provide for a transition process to make electromagnetic spectrum between the frequencies of 3700 megahertz and 4200 megahertz available for allocation and auction, to make available Federal spectrum for non-Federal use or shared Federal and non-Federal use, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Wireless Investment Now in 5G Act” or the “WIN 5G Act”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Appropriate committees of congress* The term “appropriate committees of Congress” means—
  - **(A)** the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives;
  - **(B)** the Committee on Appropriations of the Senate;
  - **(C)** the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives; and
  - **(D)** the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate.
- **(2)** *Commission* The term “Commission” means the Federal Communications Commission.

## § 3. Rule of construction

Each range of frequencies described in this Act shall be construed to be inclusive of the upper and lower frequencies in the range.
